# Design brief — Kavor

**Summary:** Kavor is a mobile-first HRM system that gives employees and HR teams one clear place for attendance, leave, payroll and workplace communication.

**Audience:** Employees using the mobile app every day; HR and operations teams choosing and administering the system.

**Brand adjectives:** Clear, dependable, modern, human, composed.

**Avoid feeling:** Bureaucratic, cold, playful-for-playful's-sake.

**Promise / big idea:** Keep people, work and important moments aligned without adding friction.

**Competitive landscape:** Productivity and HR software commonly uses blue, generic people silhouettes, check marks, briefcases and abstract multi-colour clusters. Those cues are treated as off-limits unless transformed into a distinctive form.

**Mark type hypothesis:** A compact letterform or abstract combination mark, because Kavor is a short name and the identity must work first as an app icon.

**Must work on:** 16 px favicon, app icon, mobile header, notification icon, light/dark UI and one-colour print.

**Constraints:** Preserve the current interface's emerald/mint/cream colour equity in the next phase; concept round remains black and white.

**Success criteria:** Recognisable at 16 px, clearly ownable as Kavor, calm enough for enterprise HR, warm enough for daily employee use.

**Decision-maker:** Not specified.

**Current deliverables:** Three tested concept directions and one comparison sheet. A production logo kit follows only after a direction is selected.

## Assumptions

- The public-facing product name is exactly `Kavor`, with no tagline.
- Kavor replaces or sits above the prototype name `Nhịp`; no existing logo form must be retained.
- The main market is Vietnam, with future room for broader regional use.
- The current UI palette (`#065F46`, `#A7F3D0`, `#FFF8E7`) is intentional brand equity.
- The logo should identify the product rather than illustrate every HR feature.

## Concept longlist

1. **Core K:** a bold K with a circular negative-space core — people and culture sit at the centre of the system.
2. **Relay Loop:** two mirrored hooks hand work from one to the other — continuous employee journeys and approvals.
3. **Roster K:** three independent modules align into a K — individuals become one organised workforce.
4. **Open O:** an open O becomes a portal into the employee workspace.
5. **K/V Fold:** the initials share one folded diagonal — connection between company and employee.
6. **Common Ground:** varied pillars share one baseline — different roles, one workplace.
7. **Cadence:** a repeating notch in the wordmark captures the rhythm of work.
8. **Path K:** one continuous route draws K — every HR moment in one system.
9. **Bridge:** two sides meet at a stable centre — HR as the bridge between people and organisation.
10. **Quiet Orbit:** modular elements circle an open centre — flexible teams around shared culture.

The three strongest and most different directions selected for construction are Core K, Relay Loop and Roster K.

# Kavor alternative logo concepts — round 03

This round explores three visual territories that are distinct from the soft K, orbit and handoff concepts in round 02.

## A — K Window

A rounded product tile with K cut from negative space. It is immediately usable as an app icon and remains very clear at 16 px.

**Risk:** the containing tile can feel closer to a product icon than a broad corporate identity.

## B — Gather

Three independent capsules converge around a shared centre, representing people and teams aligning without using literal human figures.

**Risk:** radial three-part marks are common and require a distinctive colour system to become ownable.

## C — KV Ligature (recommended)

K and V share one geometric structure, turning the brand name itself into the idea. This is the most distinctive and expandable direction in this round.

**Risk:** the V is a secondary discovery; some viewers will initially read only K.

## Verification

- All symbols and lockups are vector-only with no live text, raster images, filters or gradients.
- K Window and KV Ligature audit at 100/100; Gather audits at 99/100 due only to intentional optical vertical centring.
- Tested at 16–256 px, in black/white, reversed, on emerald `#065F46`, mirrored, rotated and in common product contexts.

Full production assets should be created only after a direction is selected. Trademark availability has not been checked.

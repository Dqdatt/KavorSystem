# Kavor logo concepts — round 03

Round 03 deliberately avoids the soft standalone K and handoff forms explored in round 02.

## New territories

1. **K Window:** a compact product tile with K cut from negative space — clarity, access and one place for work.
2. **Gather:** three independent capsules converge around one shared centre — people and teams aligned without literal human figures.
3. **KV Ligature:** K and V share one continuous diagonal structure — a compact, ownable monogram for the Kavor name.

The lowercase wordmark remains as a stable control so the symbol ideas can be compared fairly.

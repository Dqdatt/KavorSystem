# Kavor typographic logo concepts — round 04

Round 04 shifts from symbol-first logo design to a quieter product identity. The wordmarks are outlined from locally installed Manrope styles for exploration, with custom companion marks. A selected direction should be redrawn and optically customised before trademark use.

## A — Split K

A detached K built from three precise components, paired with a neutral lowercase wordmark. Clean, compact and immediately usable in a UI.

**Risk:** the K construction is intentionally minimal and needs colour or motion to increase ownership.

## B — Pulse

A signal line paired with a lightweight title-case wordmark. It connects subtly with employee activity and the rhythm of work.

**Risk:** pulse lines can be associated with health products, so this direction needs careful contextual testing.

## C — Status Wordmark (recommended)

A typography-led identity with a small active-status dot as the only symbolic detail. It feels closest to a modern software product rather than a traditional corporate HR logo.

**Risk:** the simplicity puts more responsibility on typography, spacing and colour; the final wordmark must be custom-drawn enough to be ownable.

## Verification

- All wordmarks are outlined SVG paths; no live text or raster content.
- Tested at 16–256 px, reversed, on emerald `#065F46`, mirrored, rotated and in common app contexts.
- Current audit scores range from 90–100/100. Remaining warnings belong to natural font-outline angles and exploratory strokes; the chosen direction will be cleaned into filled master paths.

Trademark and font-licence clearance must be confirmed before public launch.

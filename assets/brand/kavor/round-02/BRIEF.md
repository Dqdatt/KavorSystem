# Kavor logo concepts — round 02

## Feedback carried forward

Round 01 felt too heavy and conventional. Round 02 moves toward a more contemporary product identity through lighter visual weight, more negative space, softer geometry and a custom lowercase wordmark.

## Updated visual criteria

- Modern SaaS/mobile rather than traditional enterprise software.
- Calm and precise, but not rigid or institutional.
- Distinctive at app-icon size without relying on gradients or decoration.
- Rounded terminals balanced by deliberate 45° geometry.
- Lowercase `kavor` for a more human, approachable voice.

## Directions selected

1. **K Flow:** a soft K drawn as three coordinated movements rather than one solid block.
2. **K Orbit:** an open circular path becomes the spine of a K — one system around every employee moment.
3. **Handoff:** two mirrored hooks pass work forward across a shared gap.
4. **Open Wordmark:** a wordmark-led identity with an open O as its compact companion.

All artwork in this round is exploratory black-and-white SVG. Remaining strokes will be expanded only after a direction is selected.

# Kavor modern logo concepts — round 02

Round 02 responds to the request for a more contemporary identity. The visual language is lighter, softer and more product-led than round 01, with a custom lowercase `kavor` wordmark.

## Review first

- `kavor-modern-concepts.png` — comparison sheet.
- `preview-tests.html` / `preview-tests.png` — scale, reversed, rotation, background and product-context tests.
- `concept-*-symbol.svg` — standalone symbol concepts.
- `concept-*-lockup.svg` — horizontal lockups with outlined custom lettering.

## Directions

### A — K Flow (recommended)

A soft K shaped by coordinated movement. It feels human and contemporary without relying on literal people icons.

**Risk:** the organic junction needs final outline expansion and one last small-size craft pass.

### B — K Orbit

An open circular path turns into a K around one shared centre. It suggests a single system surrounding every employee moment.

**Risk:** at a quick glance it can resemble a back/rewind control.

### C — Handoff

Two mirrored hooks pass work forward across a calm open gap. It is the most abstract and product-system-oriented option.

**Risk:** the Kavor association must be learned because the symbol contains no letter.

### D — Open Wordmark

A wordmark-led direction with an open circular companion mark, prioritising the product name over a symbolic story.

**Risk:** the companion mark is deliberately neutral and would depend heavily on colour and motion for ownership.

## Verification

- Tested at 16, 32, 64, 96, 128 and 256 px.
- Tested in black, white/reversed and on the existing emerald `#065F46`.
- Tested mirrored, rotated, blurred and in browser-tab, app-icon, avatar and header contexts.
- Current audit scores: 98–99/100. Stroke-based concepts are intentionally left as editable exploration; the selected master will be expanded to filled paths.

Trademark availability is not guaranteed. Run a professional trademark and reverse-image search before launch.
